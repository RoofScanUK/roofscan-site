Quick answers

Questions people ask before booking

Anything else, see all FAQs or call 07498 364197.

How much does it cost?
It depends on the size of your roof. Prices start from £180, and you see your exact price online before you book. A £50 deposit secures the date and comes off the total.
Do I need to be home?
No. We fly from outside the property and nobody goes inside or onto the roof. You're welcome to be there if you'd like.
What if the weather is bad?
We never fly in unsafe conditions. If your slot can't go ahead, we email you a link to pick a new time at no extra cost, and your deposit carries over.
How quickly do I get the report?
Within 5 working days of the inspection, or within 24 hours with Express.
Is it legal and insured?
Yes. Our pilots hold CAA Flyer IDs, we're registered with a CAA Operator ID, and we carry public liability insurance. Copies are available on request.
Can I move my booking?
Yes, free of charge with at least 48 hours' notice, using the link in your booking email.

About RoofScan UK

Roof inspections done properly, from the air

Local, independent, and built on real construction experience.

Why we started

A normal roof inspection usually means scaffolding, ladders, cost, and risk, or a roofer climbing up and telling you what they found with nothing to back it up. RoofScan UK exists to fix that. A drone captures every tile, ridge, and flashing in high resolution, so you get a full written report you can actually trust and act on. No scaffolding, no guesswork, and nobody with a stake in what happens next.

Grounded in the trade

RoofScan UK is run by Takhy Ndiaye Marrero, who brings hands-on construction experience and a background in property to every inspection. Reports get written by someone who understands what he's looking at, not just pretty pictures, but a genuine read on what's going on up there and what to do about it.

Independent by design

We don't do the repairs, and we take no fee from anyone who does. That's on purpose. We have nothing to gain from what we find, so our reports stay honest. We tell you what we see, not what's convenient for someone else's next job.

Local to Sussex

We're based in Brighton and cover the whole of Sussex, from Chichester to Rye, the coast to the mid-Sussex villages. Being local means we know the area, the housing stock, and the weather that batters these roofs. It also means a real person answers when you call.
