How our coverage works

Our inspections are flown by local, CAA-registered pilots. Our first pilots are in Sussex and Hampshire, and we're adding pilots region by region across the UK.

When you enter your postcode we find the nearest pilot. If one covers you, you'll see your full price and the next available times. If not, you can leave your email. Every request is logged, and the areas with the most requests get pilots first.
