Why look at the roof before you exchange

On a viewing you see the roof from the street, and most of it is out of sight. Slipped slates on the rear slope, a blocked valley gutter or tired lead round a chimney rarely show from the ground.

A drone inspection photographs the roof from above and turns it into a written report you can read before you commit. Some people call this a pre-purchase roof survey. It is a visual drone inspection of the outside of the roof, not a building survey or a structural survey, and it can sit alongside any survey you arrange.

Independent by design

We inspect and report only. We never carry out repairs and we don't quote for roof work, so the report has no reason to find work that isn't there. Every pilot's CAA registration and insurance is checked before they fly for us, and we arrange the pilot for you.

What the report gives you

  • Clear drone photos of the roof slopes, ridge, valleys, chimney stacks, flashings and gutters that can be seen from the air
  • A condition rating for each part of the roof and an overall RoofScore out of 100
  • Plain-English notes on anything that may need a closer look by a roofer or a chartered surveyor
  • A clear statement of any areas the drone could not see and the limits of a visual inspection
  • The date of the inspection, so you have a record of the roof on that day

Want to see one first? Open a sample report (PDF).

What it does not cover

  • The inside of the house, the loft or the roof structure
  • Parts of the roof that cannot be seen from the drone
  • Repair specifications or repair costs
  • Valuation, legal, structural or insurance advice

If the photos suggest a deeper problem, the report says so and recommends you ask a chartered surveyor to look.
