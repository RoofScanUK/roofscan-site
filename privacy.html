Privacy Policy

This policy explains how RoofScan UK collects, uses, and protects your personal data, in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who we are

RoofScan UK is the trading name of Takhy Ndiaye Marrero, based in Brighton, East Sussex, providing drone roof inspection services across Sussex. For all data protection matters, contact: info@roofscanuk.co.uk or 07498 364197.

2. What data we collect

We collect the following personal data when you request a quote, make a booking, or engage our services:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • The address of the property to be inspected
  • Access information you provide (gate codes, parking notes, contact preferences)
  • Payment information: processed securely by Stripe; we never see or store your full card details
  • Aerial photographs and video of the property captured during the inspection
  • Records of our communications with you (emails, messages sent through our website, call notes)
  • Your booking date and time, and any changes you make to it

3. Why we collect it (legal basis)

We process your data under the following lawful bases:

  • Contract: to provide the inspection service you have booked, deliver your report, and process payment
  • Legitimate interests: to follow up quotes, request reviews after completed work, send an annual re-inspection reminder, maintain business records, and occasionally email past clients about our services, for example after a storm in your area. Every marketing email includes a one-click unsubscribe link.
  • Legal obligation: to keep financial records as required by HMRC (currently 6 years)

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for third-party marketing.

4. Drone imagery and neighbouring properties

Our inspections capture aerial imagery of the commissioned property. Due to the nature of aerial photography, neighbouring properties may incidentally appear at the edges of some images. We do not target, focus on, or retain imagery of neighbouring properties beyond what is incidental to the inspection. All flights are conducted in accordance with CAA regulations and the Air Navigation Order. If you believe your property has been incidentally captured and wish to discuss this, contact us at info@roofscanuk.co.uk.

5. Who we share data with

We share your data only with the service providers necessary to run our business:

  • Stripe: payment and deposit processing
  • Airtable and Google Workspace: job records, scheduling, and email
  • Zapier: workflow automation between the above systems
  • Xero: accounting records
  • Cal.com: booking calendar and confirmation emails
  • Cloudflare: website hosting, and secure storage of inspection photos and reports
  • CloudConvert: turning finished reports into PDF files
  • Anthropic: AI tools that help sort inspection photos and draft parts of the report. Every report is checked by a person before it is sent to you.
  • Ordnance Survey and postcodes.io: address and location lookup when you get a quote

Our inspections are carried out by self-employed, CAA-registered pilots and report writers working for RoofScan UK. They see only the details they need for your job, such as the property address, your contact details and any access notes.

We are fully independent. We do not carry out repairs, and we do not pass your details to roofers or any other contractor, or receive any fee from them. Your report is yours to share with whoever you choose.

6. How long we keep your data

  • Inspection reports and imagery: retained for 6 years, in line with our professional liability obligations
  • Financial records: 6 years, as required by HMRC
  • Quote enquiries that do not become bookings: deleted after 12 months
  • Email correspondence: reviewed and deleted periodically, retained no longer than 6 years

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to our legal retention obligations)
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent for marketing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email info@roofscanuk.co.uk. We will respond within one calendar month.

8. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always the one published on this page.
