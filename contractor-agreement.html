Contractor agreement

RoofScan UK flyers and report writers

This agreement applies to drone flyers and report writers working with RoofScan UK on a self-employed basis. You agree to it in the jobs app before claiming your first job. Questions: info@roofscanuk.co.uk.

1. Parties and status

  1. This agreement is between Takhy Ndiaye Marrero, trading as RoofScan UK ("RoofScan"), and the contractor named in their application ("you").
  2. You are self-employed. Nothing in this agreement makes you an employee or worker of RoofScan.
  3. You choose which jobs to accept. RoofScan does not guarantee any amount of work, and you may work for others, including other drone or survey businesses.
  4. You provide and maintain your own equipment, software, vehicle and phone at your own cost.

2. Flying (flyers only)

  1. You fly every job as the UAS operator under your own CAA Operator ID, and as the remote pilot under your own Flyer ID. You are responsible for the safe and lawful conduct of your flights.
  2. You comply with UK drone law and CAA rules, including airspace, distance from people and property, and any permissions a site needs.
  3. You complete the pre-flight weather, airspace and site checks in the RoofScan app, and for commercial sites the site risk assessment, before flying.
  4. You may refuse or stop any flight you judge unsafe. A safety stand-down is never treated as a missed job.
  5. You never climb onto or access a roof. Inspections are by drone only.
  6. You report any accident, injury, damage, near miss or complaint to RoofScan within 24 hours, and to the CAA or AAIB where the law requires.

3. Report writing (writers only)

  1. You write reports from the drone photos supplied, honestly and to RoofScan's report standard, describing only what the photos show.
  2. You do not state that a roof is structurally sound, or guarantee its condition, beyond what a visual inspection can support.
  3. RoofScan reviews every report before release and may ask for corrections.

4. Insurance

  1. Flyers hold, for the whole time they work with RoofScan, commercial drone third-party liability insurance in the same name as their Operator ID, and public liability insurance, at the level RoofScan sets for the work (shown on the application), or higher where a job requires it.
  2. You upload current certificates, tell RoofScan immediately if any cover lapses or changes, and do not accept or fly jobs while uninsured.
  3. The app stops you claiming jobs when your documents expire. This does not remove your duty to stay insured.

5. Responsibility

  1. Flyers: Flyers are responsible for loss, injury or damage caused by their flights, and are covered by the commercial drone insurance they must hold under section 4. A flyer indemnifies RoofScan against claims arising from their breach of this agreement or of drone law, up to the limits of that insurance.
  2. Report writers: Writers must take reasonable care and follow the report standard. RoofScan reviews every report before release and is responsible to its clients for the reports it sells. A writer is not personally liable to RoofScan for honest errors in a report that RoofScan has reviewed and released. A writer remains responsible for deliberate misconduct, dishonesty and breach of confidentiality.
  3. RoofScan: RoofScan is arranging professional indemnity insurance for the reports it sells and will tell writers in writing when it is in place.

6. Pay

  1. RoofScan pays you the amount shown on the job in the app when you accept it, calculated from RoofScan's published pay rules.
  2. RoofScan pays once a month, on the 5th (or the next weekday if the 5th is a weekend), for every job that is payable by then: flights once the client has paid, reports once RoofScan has approved them, and commercial work once the commercial client has paid its invoice.
  3. Payment is made through Stripe to the bank account you add in the app's payout setup. You must complete payout setup before claiming jobs. The app shows what you are owed for each job, broken down (for example roof scan, thermal scan, fuel), and Stripe shows each payment.
  4. You are responsible for your own tax and National Insurance.
  5. Missed jobs and late cancellations are handled under the job rules shown in the app.

7. Confidentiality, data and photos

  1. Client names, addresses, photos and reports are confidential. You use them only to do the job, keep them on your own password-protected device, and delete local copies once the job is accepted.
  2. You do not post job photos or locations on social media or elsewhere without RoofScan's written permission.
  3. Photos and reports you produce for RoofScan jobs belong to RoofScan, which licenses them to its clients.
  4. You follow RoofScan's privacy instructions under UK GDPR.

8. Clients

  1. For 12 months after your last RoofScan job, you do not offer drone inspection or report services directly to any client you met through RoofScan.

9. Ending the agreement

  1. Either side can end this agreement at any time by email. Jobs already accepted are completed or released under the app's job rules, and completed work is still paid.
  2. RoofScan can suspend your access immediately for a safety, insurance, quality or conduct concern.

10. General

  1. This agreement, the declarations in your application and the job rules in the app make up the whole agreement. RoofScan will ask you to agree again if this agreement changes.
  2. The law of England and Wales applies.
