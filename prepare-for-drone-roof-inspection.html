How to prepare for a drone roof inspection

There is very little to do. Here is what helps, what happens on the day and what the report does and does not cover.

When you book

1
Book online with the full address and postcode, and pay the £50 deposit. It comes off the total. Inspections need at least 72 hours' notice so we can plan the flight, or less with Express.
2
Tell us about anything that could affect the flight, such as overhead wires, livestock, dogs or nearby restricted airspace. Email info@roofscanuk.co.uk or call 07498 364197.
3
If you rent the property or are buying it, let the owner, the tenants or the estate agent know the date.

On the day

  • You don't need to be home. If you are, the pilot will brief you on arrival before the drone takes off.
  • Keep pets indoors during the flight.
  • No access inside the property is needed and nobody climbs or walks on your roof.
  • Expect 30 minutes to an hour on site, including setup and safety checks. Larger roofs can take a little longer.
  • Trees and other obstructions are rarely a problem. If access is genuinely restricted, the pilot will tell you on arrival before going ahead.
  • We arrange the pilot and confirm everything by email, so there is nothing to organise with the pilot yourself.

Weather

We won't fly in rain, strong wind or any conditions that break CAA safety rules or put people or property at risk. If your slot has to move because of the weather, we email you a link to pick a new time at no extra cost, and your deposit carries over.

What our pilot photographs

Our pilots follow the same checklist on every job: a shot of the front of the property from the street, high overview shots from each side, overlapping photos across every slope, then close-ups of the ridge, valleys, chimneys, flashings, edges, gutters, vents and any flat sections, extensions or outbuildings. Clean areas are photographed too, so the report can show where no issues were found.

You can read the photo checklist our pilots use.

What is covered

  • The outside of the roof as seen from the drone: coverings, ridge and hips, valleys, chimney stacks, flashings, gutters and downpipes
  • A condition rating for each part of the roof and an overall RoofScore out of 100
  • Plain-English notes on anything that may need a closer look

What is not covered

  • The inside of the property, the loft or the roof structure
  • Parts of the roof the drone cannot see, which the report lists
  • Repairs, repair specifications or repair costs. We inspect and report only.
  • Structural, valuation, legal or insurance advice

After the flight

Straight after the flight we email you a link to pay the balance. Your report follows within 5 working days of that payment, or within 24 hours of the flight with Express. It is saved in your client portal, where you can download it any time. Open a sample report (PDF).
