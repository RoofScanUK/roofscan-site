About the roof

The price is based on the roof area, which we measure from Ordnance Survey maps.

Thermal scans work best on flat roofs, where trapped water shows clearly at night. On pitched roofs they mainly show heat escaping through missing or damp insulation.

A few thermal questions

Thermal imaging needs the inside of the building warmer than outside (about 10°C or more). Without heating on, heat-loss patterns may not show. Moisture patterns on flat roofs can still show, so you can still book.

What happens next
  • Thermal scans are flown after dark, on a dry evening. The pilot picks an evening in the next 5 working days and we email you the date at least 24 hours before.
  • Please have the heating on for a few hours before, if you can. You don't need to be home.
  • The roof report photos are taken in daylight, either the same evening before dark or on a separate visit.
  • A qualified thermographer reviews the images. Your report shows each pattern found, what it is consistent with, and what to check next.
