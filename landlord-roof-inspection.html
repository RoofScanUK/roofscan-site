When landlords book

1
Before a new tenancy. A record of the roof's condition before the tenants move in.
2
At renewal or a periodic check. See what has changed since last time and plan any maintenance before it turns into a call-out.
3
After a tenant reports a leak or damp. Photos of the outside of the roof above the problem, so you can decide what to do next before you send anyone up a ladder.
4
After a storm. A quick look at the whole roof. See our storm damage checks.

A photo record you can share

Every report is dated and built around clear drone photos, with a condition rating for each part of the roof, an overall RoofScore out of 100 and plain-English notes on anything that may need attention. Any areas the drone could not see are stated in the report.

Once it is paid in full the report is yours to share with your letting agent, your insurer or a roofer. Open a sample report (PDF).

Easy for your tenants

  • Nobody needs to be home and nobody goes inside the property
  • Usually 30 minutes to an hour on site, and nobody walks on the roof
  • Let your tenants know the date and ask them to keep pets indoors during the flight
  • We arrange a vetted pilot and confirm the booking by email, so there is nothing to organise with the pilot

Independent

We inspect and report only. We never carry out repairs and we don't quote for roof work, so the report has no reason to find work that isn't there. It is a visual inspection of the outside of the roof, not a structural survey, and it does not include repair costs or legal, valuation or insurance advice.
