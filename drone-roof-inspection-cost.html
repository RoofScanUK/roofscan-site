How much does a drone roof inspection cost in the UK?

What a drone roof inspection costs in the UK in 2026, what changes the price, and the RoofScan UK prices for houses in the south of England.

Last updated 25 November 2026 · RoofScan UK

The short answer

Published UK prices for a drone roof inspection of a house mostly sit between about £200 and £500 (price guides and drone firms, checked 25 November 2026, see the table below). At RoofScan UK a terraced house starts from £255 and a detached house from £406, and you see your own price online before you book. Travel, extras and Express are added only when they apply.

RoofScan UK prices for houses

These are the prices a customer pays. RoofScan UK is not VAT registered, so there is no VAT to add. The figure online is an estimate from the roof footprint, and the final price is confirmed after the flight. It is never above the maximum shown when you book.

What changes the price

  • Roof size, from the footprint at your address.
  • Chimney stacks: +£35 each.
  • Extensions or flat roofs: +£30 each.
  • Dormers: +£20 each.
  • Outbuildings or garages: +£40 each.
  • Express report (within 24 hours of your balance payment instead of 5 working days): +£99.
  • Letting our pilot pick the day within the next 5 working days (the "Any time" option): up to £20 off, never below the £255 starting price.
  • Distance from the nearest pilot: free to 10 road miles, then 90p a mile.

A £50 deposit is taken at booking and comes off the total.

Drone roof inspection or a building survey

A drone roof inspection looks at the outside of the roof only. It does not replace a building survey, which covers the whole property. Many people book both when buying. The RoofScan UK report says plainly that it is a drone based visual inspection and not a full building survey.

What you get for the price

A written report with photos of every part of the roof, a Satisfactory, Monitor or Repair Required rating for each part, a RoofScore out of 100 and a schedule of works you can hand to a roofer. It arrives within 5 working days of your balance payment, or 24 hours with Express. We inspect and report only and do no repairs, so the report is independent.
